## Findings, most severe first

1. **Window views are not clear, and they do not read as real. This fails the observable criterion and section B.**
   - **Where:** `pull_window` in `listing_partner/segment.py`, visible on 001, 003, 004, 005, 006, 007 and 009.
   - **How I found it:** I looked at the page images, then replayed HEAD locally and looked again. The full-resolution crop of 006 shows the brick as pale pink and lavender on near-white, the foliage as white blobs, and hard jagged boundaries between dark leaves and white. 001 at HEAD still has black bands across the glass.
   - **Likely cause (from reading the code):** the pull decides per pixel, with a threshold, between three sources: the dark frame, the detail frame and the merge. No brightness or color match puts them on one scale. Gains can only darken, so the dark frame's gain usually stays at 1. The dark frame holds the threshold on only 20-43% of mask pixels (measured), so the rest comes from other sources.
   - **Contract lines missed:** "Match the pulled view's brightness and color", "no hard edges", "the view outside is clear and real".
   - **To pass:** one continuous, coherent view per window, from real frames, matched in brightness and color with feathered joins. It must read as a natural view at full resolution on all nine window photos.

2. **The near-white number passes while the glass still looks blown, so criterion 4 is met in letter and missed in spirit.**
   - **Measurement:** on 003 at HEAD, 34.2% of mask pixels are at gray 240 or above just after the pull. The metric reports 0.40% after (3,741 of 926,841).
   - **Cause:** the balance shoulder in `finish.py` compresses highlights to just under the 250 threshold. A value only reaches 250 at a gain of about 1.76 on a fully blown pixel, so the code comment saying blown windows "still read at or above 250" is false at lower gains. The builder's report says this directly ("parks just under near-white").
   - **Contract line:** criterion 4 says a result produced by clamping or capping does not count.
   - **To pass:** measure before the shoulder, or keep the shoulder away from glass, and show the numbers. Fixing finding 1 should make these numbers honest.

3. **Criterion 11 fails on 013, and the page hides it.**
   - **Measurement:** b* +18.1 vs +12.2, a gap of 5.9 against a limit of 5. I reproduced this.
   - **Cause, which the builder's report said it could not determine:** I ran set 013 through the `finish.py` from b99903e (b* +16.9, gap 4.7, passes) and through HEAD's (b* +18.1). The daylight-spill guard, commit 6dd78e4, caused the miss. That commit also changed the look after the "freeze" wording, even though no constant moved.
   - **On the page:** `build_finish_review` drops `look_ok`, so 013 shows bare numbers with no miss marked. The page has 0 FAIL lines.
   - **To pass:** 013 inside 5 units, for example by limiting the guard to interiors, and every look row showing pass or fail. Then state plainly in the report that the fit was on these same 16 photos, and hold the settings from there.
