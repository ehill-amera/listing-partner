# Hackathon rules, as published

Read from the official Participant Guide on the Cerebral Valley event page (Details tab), Oct 26, 2026.

## Rules that bind this repo

- Repositories must be public.
- Solo participants are allowed.
- The demo may only highlight features, code and functionality built during the Hackathon. Judges must be able to clearly identify what was created during the Hackathon, or the entry can be disqualified.
- What is presented must have been built during the event, and this must be clear in the submission.
- Disqualified: projects that violate legal, ethical or platform policies, or use code, data or assets without the rights to them.
- The project must incorporate Meta's models (Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe, SAM 3.1 through the Meta Model API, or the open-weight Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark Open).
- Nothing in the guide restricts which other tools may be used during development.

## Discord #rules and #faqs (read Oct 26, 2026)

- #rules: "New work only. Everything you present has to be built during the hackathon, Oct 26 to Nov 4."
- #faqs: "Correction: building period is officially after the kickoff session!" Work after the Oct 26 noon kickoff counts.
- #faqs, prior work: the submitted repo is a fresh repo with only work done during the event, and the demo video opens with one line stating what was pre-existing and what was built this week.
- #faqs: everyone involved in creating the project must be an approved participant.
- Neither #rules nor #faqs restricts development tools.
- #questions, official answer from Cerebral Valley, Oct 26, 2026, 1:36 PM ET: "Yes, that's allowed. You can build with any tools (Claude Code and Claude included), Muse Code isn't required, and there's no disclosure requirement, though noting it in your README is fine. Non-Meta models can be used alongside Meta's as long as Meta's models are a real part of what the project runs."

## Dates (Eastern)

- Kickoff Mon Oct 26, 12:00 PM. The guide lists the building period as Oct 27 to Nov 4.
- Submissions due Wed Nov 4, 9:00 PM, on the Cerebral Valley platform: public repo, three minute demo video, working product.
- Judging Nov 5 to 8. Closing ceremony Mon Nov 9, 12:00 PM (top 5 per track, live final round).

## Judging

- Stage one, pass or fail: fits a problem statement and reasonably uses Meta's models. Listing Partner fits "Build for Your World".
- Stage two, scored 0 to 10: Impact 30%, Use of Meta AI tooling 25%, Technical depth and execution 20%, Demo 25%.
- Stage three: top 5 per track, judges watch the demo videos live.
- Listed anti-projects include "simple image analyzers". The demo must show the whole listing pipeline, not one analysis step.

- Indie Developer track: $150K, $100K, $75K, $50K, $25K.
- Most Viral Project, $20K: the demo posted on X with the most engagement through Nov 8, with the post link submitted on the platform.
- Community Vote, $10K: most votes in the public gallery.
- Best Agentic Use-Case, $5K.
- Best Creative Build, $5K (Muse Image or Muse Wonder).
- Best Build on Meta Open Weight Models, $10K: back in play Oct 26 through Baseten partner inference (Muse Spark Open), decided by the navigator with Erik's go-ahead.
